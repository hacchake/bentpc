Introduction: what is a circuit-bent toy?

Welcome to the world of broken sound!

This is a rack of children's toy instruments picked up at a junk market and modded to the extreme: circuit-bent toys!

Deliberately rewiring a toy's circuits and adding buttons and knobs to get sounds its makers never imagined is calledcircuit bending. This app builds 7 of those modded toys (sampler included) entirely inside your computer. Unlike the real thing, you can't break them and you can't get a shock!

1. BLIPPY BOOK 30

It talks! It sings! It gets it wrong! The modded toy PC

A children's computer with a screen in the lid and 26 letter keys plus do-re-mi number keys. Its 8 modes read letters, words, play melodies, piano, drums, sound effects and a quiz. Add GLITCH buttons, LOOP, STRETCH and DIST, and you get a modded machine whose voice falls apart!

2. PIKOTONE PT-32

Lower the voltage and both sound and soul start to wobble

A 32-key mini keyboard with a rhythm machine and 8 sounds (ORCHESTRA). A separate effects unit sits on top, and on the left we added voltage-starve knobs (deliberately cutting the power) and touch points. A proper modded machine!

3. SPIN-TOT DJ-28

Scratch it! Stop it! Shade it!

A children's DJ set: scratch the disc, 6 sound-effect pads, a 13-key keyboard and 28 rhythms (7 of them hidden). We added coarse/fine pitch, a tape-stop STOP, a light sensor, two DISTs and FEEDBACK. A modded machine!

4. VROOMBOX VR-5

Play the engine's explosions as a scale!

A children's driving dashboard (our own design). The engine sound is made of "ignition grains": skip some with the mod panel and they become rhythms, and TUNE turns them into a scale. Its pride: you can play the engine itself from MIDI or the sequencer!

5. TYPOTRON TT-109

Play the keyboard as an instrument!

A PC keyboard turned into an instrument (our own design). Every key has a job: the letter rows are scales, the numpad is drums, and the F keys pick waveforms, scales and "keyboard faults". With 12 extra knobs on top, it's a modded machine!

6. TELEKEY TK-6

Wreck the picture and the sound together!

A modded keyboard with a CRT monitor (our own design). It captures picture and sound from a webcam, another tab or a video file and wrecks them with keys. The small text under each key is "picture effect / sound effect". One-shot GLITCH × BASE glitches, 8 knobs and heat runaways: a video machine!

7. PAKU-PAKU 16

Eat sounds, chop them, arrange them!

A sampler made by modding a children's recording toy (with a crocodile-mouth speaker). Fill 16 pads × 16 banks with recorded voices, loaded sounds and the 32 built-in ones, then hit them. In the rack and studio this small version plays, records and auto-composes; sound building (recording, chopping, effects, bending, patterns) happens on its own page (see "The sampler").

Uncle Mod

Each one breaks in its own way: "reads words wrong", "wobbles from too little power", "plays scales with an engine"... Learning each toy's quirks one at a time is the fastest way to get good!

★ Key point!

Switch toys with the tabs at the top (1–7). Hidden toys keep playing, so you can layer them.STUDIO lets you line up your favourite toys and make songs together.

    💡 PC Z–/ (white keys), A… (black keys), Q–P (upper white keys), 2–9 (black keys)

    AMP POWER / CPU POWER

    Voltage starve. Lowering AMP makes the sound quieter, choppy and shaky. Lowering CPU makes everything slower, lower and wobblier; go further and sounds mutate, sometimes stop, and even the notes you play drift (the rhythm too).

    💡 The heart of this machine! Lower it slowly

  11. 11

    AMP TOUCH 1–3

    Only while your finger is on a silver dot: 1 = hum, 2 = distortion, 3 = squealing oscillation. Move up/down while pressing to change the strength.

  12. 12

    Bends the pitch only while your finger is on the silver dot. Move up/down while pressing to change the strength.

  13. 13

    💡 PC Tab for ENV / HOLD

  14. 14

    DIST = distortion, FIZZ = fizzy noise, HIPASS = cuts the lows (RESO adds a twangy resonance).

  15. 15

    FEEDBACK (LONG)· PITCH

    FEEDBACK = the sound loops back on itself (SHORT = 4 ms buzz, LONG = 280 ms echo). PITCH = with the switch on, shifts everything ±1 octave.

4VROOMBOX VR-5Play the engine's explosions as a scale!

A children's driving dashboard (our own design). The engine sound is made of "ignition grains": skip some with the mod panel and they become rhythms, and TUNE turns them into a scale. Its pride: you can play the engine itself from MIDI or the sequencer!

  1. 1

    POWER (ignition key)

    Click to switch on (the key turns to ON). Keep clicking and the starter cranks until the engine starts. KEY OFF switches it off.

    💡 PC Enter (the starter cranks while held)

  2. 2

    VOLUME

    Volume.

  3. 3

    GEAR

    N, 1–5. The higher the gear, the slower the revs catch up. The revs jump the moment you shift.

    💡 PC Z / X

  4. 4

    ACCEL (pedal)

    Throttle while pressed. Revs (= ignition rate = pitch) go up.

    💡 PC ↑ or W

  5. 5

    Steering · Horn

    Grab the rim to turn it (it springs back). The centre is the horn.

    💡 PC ← →, Space for the horn

  6. 6

    Radio

    Presets 1–8 tune the station. During PRESET HIJACK the preset buttons become an engine scale (C to C).

    💡 PC 1–8, R changes station

  7. 7

    TURBO

    Turbo while held. Revs shoot up.

    💡 PC T

  8. 8

    SIREN / SIGNAL / WIPERS / CRASH!

    Siren, ticking indicator, wipers (on/off); CRASH! is a crash sound.

    💡 PC S / V / B / C

  9. 9

    FIRING ORDER 1–8 · CAM

    Switches for 8 cylinders. Skip ignitions and low revs turn into rhythms. CAM sets the pattern length (3–8).

    💡 The heart of this machine! Skip some, then just a little throttle

  10. 10

    REDLINE · SPARK · RADIO BLEED

    REDLINE = raises the rev limit (at 0 the limiter skips ignitions; higher revs higher), SPARK = a crackle on each ignition, RADIO BLEED = radio and engine multiply together.

  11. 11

    TURBO FB

    When on, it feeds back the sound one ignition late, making a metallic ring that follows the revs.

  12. 12

    TUNE · HIJACK · HORN BEND · GRIND

    TUNE = revs snap to a scale, HIJACK = play the engine with the radio buttons, HORN BEND = the horn bends, GRIND = grinding on every gear change.

    💡 PC U for TUNE, H for HIJACK

  13. 13

    STARTER LOOP

    While held, the starter motor keeps cranking even with the engine running.

    💡 PC Backspace

  14. 14

    CHASSIS / HAZARD

    Touch the metal dots: CHASSIS = adds hum and sometimes skips ignitions. HAZARD = chops the engine finely with more relay clicks.

A PC keyboard turned into an instrument (our own design). Every key has a job: the letter rows are scales, the numpad is drums, and the F keys pick waveforms, scales and "keyboard faults". With 12 extra knobs on top, it's a modded machine!

    Line-loop speed (60–240 BPM) and pitch-bend range (0–12 semitones).

  6. 6

    GHOST · SCAN RATE · BOUNCE · CLICK

    Strength of the "keyboard faults" (how often GHOST fires, SCAN speed, BOUNCE count) and the volume of the key clicks.

  7. 7

    Which of POWER, LOOP, LATCH, OVR, STUT, CORRUPT, GHOST, SCAN, BOUNCE, OVERFLOW are active.

  9. 9

    F5–F8: SCALE

    MAJOR, MINOR, PENTA, BENT (a deliberately detuned scale).

  10. 10

    F9–F12: keyboard faults

  12. 12

  13. 13

    KICK · SNARE · HAT · OPEN · TOM L · TOM H · CLAP · COW · ZAP · CRASH.

★ Tips for riding the edge
  • Tap glitches short and on the beat rather than holding them. Less heat, and it grooves.
  • When the runaway starts, release just one. Heat depends a lot on how many you hold.
  • The toy PC warns you with a "Too hot!" bubble. On TELEKEY, keep an eye on the HEAT LED.
  • Runaways are part of the performance! If you like it, stay hot on purpose.

Real crashes: the sequencer's CRASH

Put a CRASH→reboot note on the sequencer's ⚡ system row to crash on purpose for its length. The auto-composer puts one in the break when CHAOS is high, and always at the end in the "Collapse" style.

How to recover

  1. RESET: on the toy PC, Esc or the RESET button. The CPU stops and the toy is off (a bubble tells you).
  2. POWER: press POWER (or Enter) again to start up. When you hear "HELLO", it's back!
  3. A sequencer CRASH resets and reboots automatically when the note ends. To stop it, press ■ (stop).
⚠ Careful!

If the toy PC's LOOP switch is at the bottom (MUTE), it won't start after RESET even if you press POWER. Move the switch up or to the middle.

Troubleshooting (Q&A)

Don't panic, read this

Q. No sound!
A. First check that POWER is on (the LED by the button glows green). Browsers won't play sound until you interact with the page, so pressing POWER or Enter also gets the sound ready. Check the volume knob, your computer's volume and that the browser tab isn't muted. The toy PC makes no sound with LOOP at MUTE (bottom).
Q. Keys don't respond
A. If you touch it while off, the POWER button flashes to tell you. In the studio the keyboard goes to the toy you clicked (green frame). After clicking the sequencer, the keyboard belongs to the sequencer, so click the toy again. The toy PC ignores keys until its startup "HELLO" finishes.
Q. The F keys don't work
A. Browsers sometimes grab F6, F7, F11, F12 and so on first. If so, click the on-screen keys with the mouse.
Q. No picture (TELEKEY)
A. The CRT stays dark until you switch on. Without camera permission you get TEST (test video). If TAB says "no audio", you forgot to turn on "Also share tab audio" in the share dialog.
Q. The screen is all white / black
A. TELEKEY uses WebGL2. If the monitor says "WebGL2 is not available", check that "hardware acceleration" is on in your browser settings or try a recent Chrome. Reloading the page (F5) helps too.
Q. It won't come back from a crash
A. RESET stops the toy PC's CPU, so start it with POWER. A sequencer CRASH resets and reboots automatically when its note ends. If it doesn't, press ■ (stop). If it is just running hot, let go and it cools down.
Q. The same URL gives a different song
A. When the auto-composer program is updated, the same settings can give a different song. On the same version of the page it is always the same.
Q. Where are my songs saved?
A. Automatically inside this browser (not on other computers or browsers). "Save" in the sequencer makes a JSON file; "Load" brings it back.
Q. Does it work on phones and tablets?
A. It opens, though small. But this app is built around playing with a PC keyboard, so Chrome on a PC is the best choice. (The PAKU-PAKU 16 sampler page is designed for phones.)

Glossary

Hard words in one line

circuit bending
Deliberately rewiring toys and electronics or adding parts to get sounds their designers never imagined. This app imitates that inside your computer.
Heavy mod
Adding lots of buttons and knobs through circuit bending.
Glitch
Sound or picture breakups that look like a "bug". Here you cause them on purpose for fun.
BASE
The knob that switches glitch families. The same button gives a different glitch when BASE changes.
LFO
A slowly swinging wave (Low Frequency Oscillator). It wobbles pitch or strength automatically.
Loop / LOOP HOLD
Grabbing part of a sound and repeating it.
Feedback
Feeding the output (sound or picture) back in. The source of echoes, howling and runaways.
Distortion (DIST)
Deliberately distorting the sound.
Bit crush (CRUSH)
Lowering the resolution (bits and rate) for a gritty old-video-game sound.
Voltage starve
Deliberately starving it of power. The sound gets slow, low and wobbly, and finally stops.
Heat (stress)
An invisible value that rises when you overdo it. Too high and glitches run wild by themselves (runaway). Let go to cool down.
Crash
During a sequencer CRASH the sound sticks and the screen freezes. Afterwards it RESETs and reboots.
Sequencer
Records your playing (which keys when, how the knobs moved) and plays it back automatically.
Overdub
Recording more on top of what you already recorded.
Automation
Recording knob moves and replaying them. In the sequencer: the ◠ knob rows.
Section
A part of a song (intro, verse, chorus...).
BPM
Beats per minute: the speed of a song.
Seed
The "seed" of the random numbers. The same seed gives the same random sequence, so you get the same song and glitches.
Motif
A short phrase repeated in a song. The auto-composer repeats a 2-bar motif with small changes.
Cue points
A fixed point in a video. Press a key to jump there.
CRT
An old TV screen. TELEKEY's screen is made to look rounded and blurry like one.
MIDI
The standard for connecting electronic instruments. Keyboards and sequencers send each other data.
WAV / WebM
WAV is an audio file, WebM is a video + audio file.
